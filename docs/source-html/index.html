Portales doesn't have a community theater. We'd like to fix that.

A few neighbors have started meeting about it. The idea is straightforward: build an organization that belongs to this town, and put a first production on stage in spring 2027.

Nothing is settled yet — not the venue, not the show, not the money. We're telling people now because a theater made by one small group isn't the point. It has to be made by enough of us.

What we're proposing

A community theater is a company made of the people who live here. Not touring professionals — teachers, students, retirees, shift workers, anyone who wants to build a set or run a light board or stand on a stage for the first time at forty-five.

Roosevelt County has the audience for it. It has a university with a theater program and people who already know how this work is done. What it doesn't have is a company outside the university that anyone in town can join.

We want to start one, prove it with a single production, and build it to last past that first show. A theater that closes after opening night isn't a theater. It's an event.

Ways to be part of it

Help start it

We need people who'll sit in the founding meetings — and people who know bookkeeping, grant writing, carpentry, or how to run a rehearsal calendar.

Support it

A first production costs real money before it earns any. Local businesses and individual supporters are how a founding season gets paid for.

Perform or crew

Auditions aren't scheduled yet. Tell us you're interested and you'll hear when they are — no experience expected.

Talk to us

Whether you want to help build it, support it, be on stage, or just ask what this is — write to us. Someone will answer.
